### Brand direction
The supplied logo combines a deep navy monogram and wordmark with a muted gold upward element and fine gold rules on a white background. The website should carry this same restrained, premium and dependable visual language.

### Core colour palette

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|

| Advisory Gold | `#C4A35A` | Highlights, rules, icons and selected headings |

| Cloud Grey | `#F4F6F9` | Cards, forms and supporting sections |
| Charcoal | `#263247` | Body copy and high-readability text |

### Typography and interface
- **Headings:** Aptos Display, Avenir Next, Montserrat, or a similar clean geometric sans serif.
- **Body:** Aptos, Inter, or Source Sans 3 for accessibility and readability.
- **Buttons:** navy background with white text; gold hover or outline treatment.
- **Photography:** real Australian business environments, professional workspaces, finance discussions and dashboard use. Avoid calculators, piggy banks, staged handshakes and call-centre imagery.
- **Layout:** generous white space, short content blocks, clear outcomes, restrained gold accents and strong conversion prompts.
